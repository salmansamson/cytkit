# Amplified photodiode 

>i (For Cytkit 1S-1F only. Not required for Cytkit 2S-14F.)

The recommended photodiode module is the Thorlabs PDA10 or PDA10A2, which is an amplified silicon photodiode with its own power supply and BNC output. This may be used for FSC as occultation (light loss) or small angle light scatter.
